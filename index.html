A Letter For You

There is something I want to tell you...

My Dear,

Happy Birthday to the most beautiful person in my life.

Maybe things between us are not the same anymore. We have been through a lot, and even though we are no longer together, I’m genuinely grateful that we can still talk, laugh, and stay connected as friends.

There’s something I don’t always say, but maybe today is the right day to say it — I still care about you, more deeply than you probably realize. Maybe I never really found the right words to explain how much you still mean to me. And I don’t expect anything from you by saying this. I just wanted you to know.

I’m also genuinely happy seeing how your life is changing. You spent so much time in the village, almost like you were stuck inside four walls, and now you’ve finally stepped into a completely new chapter. You’ve gone to Dhaka, got admitted to college, and now you have the freedom to experience life, meet new people, make new memories, and discover yourself in ways you couldn’t before.

I hope you enjoy this new life to the fullest. Make some beautiful friendships, especially with girls who make you feel comfortable, understood, and happy. And when it comes to new people and new friendships, especially with boys, just be a little thoughtful and careful about whom you trust. Not because you need someone to control your life, but because you deserve friendships that respect you, your boundaries, and your peace.

Dhaka is going to bring you many new experiences, and I hope most of them become beautiful memories for you. I hope college gives you good friends, wonderful opportunities, endless laughter, and a version of yourself that feels happier and more free.

And wherever life takes you, I hope you never forget that there is someone who genuinely wants to see you happy, successful, safe, and smiling—even if our relationship has changed.

I may not have a place in your life the way I once did, but my feelings for you are still real. Maybe time will change many things, but I’ll always be thankful for the memories, the conversations, the laughter, and every little moment we shared.

So on your birthday, I don’t want to ask you for anything. I just want to wish you a beautiful new beginning.

Keep smiling. Keep growing. Enjoy your new life. And please, always take care of yourself.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for existing. And most importantly, thank you for being a part of my story.

With love,
Someone who still cares about you more than you know. ❤️
